# 2026-11-08 — XENO Audience: what it is, and what it reuses

**Status:** proposed, for the owner. Audience ("Newsletters and memberships", Social › Audience) has a slot in the
workspace and no repo, spec or catalog entry. Creating a product is the owner's call.

## Context

What it would need already exists in pieces we own: **XENO Mail** sends mail (its server is offline today), **XENO
Post** publishes to networks and has approvals and scheduling, **Corpo › Customers** holds contacts with GDPR export
and erase, **Forms** collects sign-ups, **XENO Sites** (proposed) would host the archive and sign-up page, and the
**credit ledger / Stripe** would take membership payments.

## How the leaders do it

**Substack / Beehiiv / Ghost**: write once, send as email and publish as a web post; subscribers, free and paid
tiers, an archive site. **Mailchimp / Kit**: lists, segments, automations, deliverability tooling. **Patreon**:
memberships first, posts second.

## Options

1. **Audience is a front-end, not a new backend:** subscribers are Customers contacts with a consent record; an issue
   is written once and sent through XENO Mail (bulk-sending infrastructure there: unsubscribe headers, bounces,
   suppression list), published as a web post through Sites, and scheduled/approved like Post. Paid memberships wait
   for sales to open.
2. **A standalone product (its own repo)** with its own subscriber store and sending.
3. **Not now.**

## Recommendation

**Option 1, after XENO Mail's server is back and Sites exists.** It follows the owner's rule that workspace areas
front products we already have, and it adds no second contact store (one list per person, one GDPR erase). Until
then the area stays "Coming soon", which is true.

## Blast radius

Mail gains bulk sending with consent and suppression (deliverability is the real work, and it is legally one-way:
consent records must be kept). Customers gains a consent record per contact (additive).

## What I need from you

Whether Audience is a product at all, and if so yes or no on Option 1.
