Background uploads

Save and move on. Quitting never loses a change.

What you save to a drive uploads in the background. Anything still waiting is kept on disk when the app closes, and picks up where it stopped the next time the drive mounts. Try it: save a few files, then quit halfway.

XENO Connect

Your Google credentials never land on this PC.

XENO Connect holds the access you approved in your browser. For each thing the drive does, XENO Storage asks Connect for short-lived access to do just that, and keeps nothing it could reuse. Pick an action to watch the trip.
