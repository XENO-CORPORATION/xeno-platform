We've made the **entire Video Studio Canvas completely FREE** to use. You can:

- ✨ **Create unlimited projects** - No credit cost
- ✨ **Edit projects** - Unlimited edits
- ✨ **Save projects** - Auto-save every 2 seconds
- ✨ **Load projects** - Access all your saved work

### 💰 Only Rendering Costs Credits

The **ONLY** time you use credits is when you click the final **"Render"** button to export your video.

#### Credit Cost Formula
```
credits = (width × height × duration × fps) / 1,000,000
```

#### Examples:
| Resolution | Duration | FPS | Credits |
|------------|----------|-----|---------|
| 720p (1280×720) | 5s | 30fps | ~115 |
| 1080p (1920×1080) | 10s | 24fps | ~500 |
| 1080p (1920×1080) | 10s | 60fps | ~1,250 |
| 4K (3840×2160) | 10s | 30fps | ~2,500 |
| 4K (3840×2160) | 10s | 60fps | ~5,000 |

## How It Works

### Before Rendering (FREE)
1. Click "Canvas" button → **FREE**
2. Create new project → **FREE**
3. Configure settings (resolution, FPS, quality) → **FREE**
4. Add videos, images, audio to timeline → **FREE**
5. Edit, rearrange, add effects → **FREE**
6. Save your work → **FREE** (auto-saves every 2s)
7. Close and come back later → **FREE**
8. Load your project again → **FREE**
9. Make more changes → **FREE**
10. Repeat steps 4-9 as many times as you want → **FREE**

### When Rendering (COSTS CREDITS)
11. Click "Render" button → **Shows credit cost**
12. Confirm dialog shows:
    - 💵 Credits needed
    - 💰 Your available credits
    - 💵 Credits remaining after render
13. If you confirm → **Credits deducted**
14. Video renders and saves → **Final video delivered**

## Best Practices

### 1. Experiment First
- Create your project
- Try different settings
- Edit and refine
- Preview your work
- **All FREE!**

### 2. Preview Before Rendering
- Make sure everything looks perfect
- Check timeline alignment
- Verify audio sync
- Review effects
- **All FREE!**

### 3. Optimize Settings
Before rendering, optimize for your needs:
- **Lower resolution** if quality isn't critical → Fewer credits
- **Lower FPS** for simpler content → Fewer credits
- **Shorter duration** by trimming → Fewer credits
- **Medium quality** instead of ultra → Fewer credits

### 4. Start Small
- Test with a short clip first (5s @ 720p = ~115 credits)
- Verify the output quality
- Then render the full version
