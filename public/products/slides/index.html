Features

Slide Engine

Canvas/WebGPU-powered rendering for smooth editing and playback. Infinite canvas with snapping, guides, and alignment tools.

Transitions & Animations

Entrance, exit, emphasis, and motion path animations at 60fps. Slide transitions with timing and trigger controls.

Presenter Mode

Dual-screen presenter view with speaker notes, slide preview, timer, and annotation tools for live presentations.

Templates & Themes

Professional slide templates and master layouts. Consistent branding with reusable color schemes and typography.

AI Slide Generation

Generate entire presentations from an outline or topic. AI suggests layouts, content, and design improvements.

Shape Engine

Rectangles, ellipses, arrows, connectors, and freeform paths. Rich text frames with inline formatting.

Media Embedding

Embed images, videos, and audio directly on slides. Crop, resize, and apply effects inline.

PPTX Compatibility

Import and export PowerPoint files with formatting preservation. Seamless interchange with existing workflows.

PDF Export

Export slide decks to PDF with print-ready quality. Configurable slide layout and handout formats.
