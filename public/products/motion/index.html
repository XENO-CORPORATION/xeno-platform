Features

Timeline Editor

Multi-track timeline with drag-and-drop, trimming, splitting, and snapping. Unlimited video and audio tracks.

Color Grading

Professional color wheels, curves, LUT support, and scopes. Non-destructive color pipeline with real-time preview.

Visual Effects

Compositing with blend modes, masks, and keyframe animation. Built-in effects library with GPU acceleration.

Motion Graphics

Text animation, shape layers, keyframe editor, and expression system. Create titles, lower thirds, and overlays.

AI Editing

Auto-cut detection, scene segmentation, AI color match, and smart reframing. Speed up your editing workflow.

Audio Mixing

Multi-track audio with volume keyframes, EQ, compression, and effects. Waveform visualization and sync tools.

Export Options

H.264, H.265, ProRes, and more. Custom presets for YouTube, social media, cinema, and broadcast delivery.

Format Support

Import MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WebM, ProRes, and image sequences. Native project format with full undo history.

GPU Rendering

Hardware-accelerated playback and rendering with NVIDIA CUDA and NVENC. Real-time preview at full resolution.
