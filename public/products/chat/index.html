Features

Channels & Threads

Organize conversations by topic with channels. Keep discussions focused with threaded replies.

AI Search

Semantic search across all conversations. Find messages by meaning, summarize threads, and get AI-generated answers.

File Sharing

Share files, images, and documents inline. Preview attachments without leaving the conversation.

Voice Messages

Record and send voice messages. AI transcription makes them searchable and accessible.

End-to-End Encryption

Optional E2E encryption for sensitive conversations. Signal protocol integration for maximum privacy.

Reactions & Polls

React to messages with emojis. Create polls for quick team decisions and feedback.

Integrations

Connect with XENO Mail, Calendar, and Meet. Bot framework for custom automations.

Offline Support

Read and compose messages offline. Automatic sync when reconnected.

Self-Hosted

Run your own server or use peer-to-peer mode. Your messages, your infrastructure, your rules.
