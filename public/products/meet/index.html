XENO Meet

Video conferencing with AI meeting intelligence. HD video calls, screen sharing, recording, live transcription, and automatic meeting summaries.

Download v0.2.0 Release Notes

Windows 10+ · Free · Open Source

Features

HD Video Calls

Up to 4K resolution with adaptive bitrate. Low-latency WebRTC connections for crisp, real-time communication.

Screen Sharing

Share your entire screen, a specific window, or a browser tab. Remote control for collaborative troubleshooting.

Recording

Record meetings locally with separate audio tracks per participant. No cloud storage limits or retention policies.

Live Transcription

Real-time speech-to-text powered by local AI. Speaker identification and timestamped transcript export.

AI Meeting Notes

Automatic meeting summaries, action items, and key decisions extracted by AI. Shared to all participants.

Virtual Backgrounds

AI-powered background blur and replacement. Custom backgrounds without green screen hardware.

Breakout Rooms

Split large meetings into smaller groups. Automatic or manual assignment with timed sessions.

Calendar Integration

One-click join from XENO Calendar events. Automatic meeting links and reminders.

Self-Hosted

Run your own TURN/STUN server for complete control. No third-party infrastructure required.
