Learn how to set up and use XENO Meet for video conferencing.

Getting Started

  • Starting a meeting — Click New Meeting to create an instant call. Share the link with participants or schedule via Calendar.
  • Screen sharing — Click the Share button to share your screen, a window, or a browser tab. Grant remote control if needed.
  • Transcription — Enable live transcription from Settings. AI transcribes in real-time with speaker labels.
  • Recording — Click Record to save the meeting locally. Separate audio tracks per participant for post-production.
