Features

Multi-Track Recording

Record unlimited audio tracks with low-latency monitoring. ASIO support for professional audio interfaces.

Mixing Console

Full mixing board with faders, pan, sends, busses, and master channel. Real-time metering and level monitoring.

Effects Processing

Built-in EQ, compressor, reverb, delay, chorus, and more. VST plugin support for third-party effects.

Audio Editing

Non-destructive editing with cut, copy, paste, crossfade, and time-stretch. Spectral view for detailed editing.

Mastering Tools

Multiband compressor, limiter, stereo imager, and loudness metering. LUFS-compliant mastering workflow.

AI Audio Tools

AI noise reduction, voice separation, audio enhancement, and speech-to-text. All models run locally.

MIDI Support

MIDI recording, editing, and playback. Piano roll editor with quantization, velocity editing, and CC automation.

Automation

Automate any parameter with curves, breakpoints, and drawing tools. Read, write, and latch recording modes.

Format Support

WAV, FLAC, MP3, OGG, AIFF, and more. Up to 32-bit float, 192kHz sample rates. Batch export capabilities.

Recommended (AI Features)

  • 16 GB RAM
  • ASIO-compatible audio interface
