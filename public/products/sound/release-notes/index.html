v0.2.0 March 2026

Ecosystem Integration & Corpo Suite

All 15 XENO apps now available through Hub. Added corpo suite integration, improved update detection, cross-app workflow support, and performance improvements.

v0.1.0 March 2026

Initial Release

The first public release of XENO Sound brings a complete digital audio workstation with AI-powered audio tools.

Recording & Editing

  • + Multi-track recording with unlimited tracks
  • + ASIO low-latency audio driver support
  • + Non-destructive editing with full undo history
  • + Waveform and spectral view editing

Mixing & Effects

  • + Full mixing console with faders, pan, and sends
  • + Built-in EQ, compressor, reverb, delay, and chorus
  • + Parameter automation with curve editor
  • + Bus routing and aux sends

AI Features

  • + AI noise reduction and audio cleanup
  • + Voice/music separation (stem splitting)
  • + Speech-to-text transcription
  • + All AI runs locally via XENO Lib

Format Support

  • + Import/Export: WAV, FLAC, MP3, OGG, AIFF
  • + Up to 32-bit float, 192kHz sample rates
  • + MIDI import and editing
