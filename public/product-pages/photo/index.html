RAW photo workflow

Cull fast.
Develop without a trace.

XENO Photo is a photographer-first workspace for importing, culling and developing RAW images non-destructively, with denoise, upscale and subject masking running locally. This is a concept demo: rate frames with 1–5, then develop one.

The heavy AI runs on your machine.

Denoise, upscale and subject masking are planned to run on the shared XENO inference runtime, so your RAW files never have to leave the disk.
