XENO Meet streams one machine’s display to another at native resolution and frame rate, over your private Tailscale mesh, at a bitrate you choose. It is for two computers, not for meetings. Change the link below and see why a fixed bitrate matters.

What you’ll need

Two Windows PCs, two NVIDIA GPUs, one tailnet.

Version 0.1 captures with DXGI and encodes and decodes on NVIDIA hardware through xeno-lib. macOS and Linux are out of scope for 0.1.

Windows 11On the host and the viewer
NVIDIA GPUWith NVENC and NVDEC; the driver is enough, no CUDA toolkit
TailscaleBoth machines on the same tailnet
Two peersOne screen, direct. No rooms, invites or presence

Looking for video calls? That’s XENO Comms. If Meet ever grows rooms or a third participant, the two merge.
